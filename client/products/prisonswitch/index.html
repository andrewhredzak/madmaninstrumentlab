Core Functionality

Control Beyond Basic On-Off

  • Switch operations: supports hold, on-off, and expression functionality, with a potential double-click feature for new modes.
  • Performance monitoring: hold both switches down to view pedal statistics, including how many times the pedal has been switched or how long it has been held.
  • Microcontroller power: ESP32-S3 family architecture showcases advanced microcontroller skills and future-proof wireless integration.
  • Easter egg: hold the switch for 10 seconds to discover the hidden strobe effect.
Prison Variant Design Ethos

Sophisticated, No-Blinking Aesthetic

  • Transparent housing: the flagship Prison variant uses a completely clear acrylic housing that exposes the internal digital circuitry and mounted components.
  • Premium materials: the enclosure is accented with brushed metals and set in brushed steel screws.
  • Visual feedback: status is provided discreetly through a transparent 1.51-inch blue OLED screen with 128x64 resolution and SSD1309 driver chip.
  • Profile: designed with a low, flat profile.
